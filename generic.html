My Profile

Where spatial thinking meets community impact.

I'm Jack Kgwale — a Geography and Environmental Science graduate from the University of Pretoria with a passion for how data, maps, and people converge to build more resilient communities. My work sits at the intersection of technical GIS expertise and on-the-ground community engagement, using spatial tools to tackle real-world challenges from disaster preparedness to long-term environmental monitoring.

Through my leadership in youth-led initiatives like WAFARI — a project that has raised flood awareness across multiple African countries — I've seen firsthand how maps can be instruments of advocacy and change, not just technical outputs. I've led teams, designed surveys, co-organised mapathons, and brought spatial storytelling to community audiences and policymakers alike.

What Drives Me

  • 🔬 Evidence-based action: I ground every campaign, map, and recommendation in real data — collected through fieldwork, surveys, and satellite imagery.
  • 📣 Youth advocacy: I believe young people are not just the future of environmental action — we are its present. I've built and led programmes that prove it.
  • 🧩 Continuous learning: From open-source GIS to biodiversity monitoring platforms, I actively seek new tools and perspectives that sharpen my impact.
  • 🌍 African context: My work is rooted in understanding the specific climate vulnerabilities, governance structures, and community dynamics of the African continent.
